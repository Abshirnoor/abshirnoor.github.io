Early Life & Education

Hello there! My name is Abshir Nor Ali, a Bachelor of Computer Science graduate from the University of Somalia (UNISO). Growing up in Mogadishu, Somalia during a time of intense conflict, my family was forced to relocate multiple times, facing numerous challenges such as limited access to education and safety. Despite these obstacles, I was fortunate to attend school, an opportunity some of my older siblings did not have.

Throughout my studies, I have been deeply interested in digital innovation. In high school, I took basic computer training, where I learned how to use a computer and developed excellent typing skills. After finishing school, I studied computer science for four years at the University of Somalia.

Freelancing & Early Career

I have dedicated myself to learning and exploring various aspects of technology whenever possible. In 2019, I started working on freelance marketplaces and collaborating with both online platforms and local startups.

Through my freelancing network, I came across a startup called Rise Academy, which is a coding school where I met a group of 15 Somali girls who aspired to become web developers. Although they had acquired technical skills, they didn't know how to apply them professionally. In response, I developed a freelancing course that is now part of the academy's curriculum.

Humanitarian Work with Tarjimly

In my third year at the university, I connected with a U.S.-based NGO called Tarjimly through one of my freelance channels. They needed experienced English-Somali translators/interpreters for humanitarian work. In this role, I worked with healthcare providers, attorneys, educators, and aid workers by breaking language barriers worldwide and facilitating access to essential services.

Through Tarjimly, I supported refugee and immigrant communities by providing interpretation and translation services in medical, legal, and educational contexts. I completed over 300 sessions with an average rating of 4.67/5, earning a Certificate of Junior Volunteer and deepening my commitment to making a humanitarian impact.

Innovation Hub Experience

A significant step in my tech journey came when I joined iRiseHub, Somalia's first innovation hub. Before joining, I had the opportunity to meet the team at the Mogadishu Tech Summit, an annual gathering that connects Somalia's digital innovators to the rest of the world.

During our discussions, I learned about their challenges with social media engagement and visibility for the summit. I proposed a solution: integrating an advanced intelligence tool to streamline and enhance their content posting, thereby boosting the summit's social media presence. This initiative was successful, leading to significant engagement on platforms like Twitter and Facebook.

This was a turning point, leading to an internship at iRiseHub Limited, where I maintained and improved websites to enhance organizational visibility through digital content. One of my key contributions was transitioning from on-premises base applications to cloud-based solutions, which significantly increased its online presence and boosted community engagement from 8,000 to 40,000 active social media users.

International Expansion

My professional journey has since expanded beyond Somalia. Moving to Ghana introduced me to diverse cultures and new technological environments. While working at Teleperformance, I was exposed to AI technologies, which deepened my understanding of their practical applications in solving real-world problems.
